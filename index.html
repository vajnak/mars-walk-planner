Mars Walk Planner

Plan a safe walk across real Martian terrain, then step into it in first person.

Map and route plannerLayers, slope hazards, and a walking route that respects your slope limit. Walk it in first person3D terrain with a visor-style display. Best in Chrome on a laptop.

What is this?

A prototype for NASA Space Apps 2026 (Interplanetary Survival Guide). It uses a 2 km square of the Jezero Crater region, built from real HiRISE elevation and imagery. Slopes and routes are calculated from the elevation data.

Data

NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona HiRISE DTM (DTEEC_048842_1985_048908_1985_U01) and orthoimage (ESP_048842_1985_RED_C_01), distributed by USGS Astrogeology.
